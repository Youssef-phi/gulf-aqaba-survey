Gulf-Aqaba Survey

Four tiles glide toward you, one per answer. Steer the ball into the lane of the right answer before the tiles arrive.

How to play

  1. You start with 200 points. Before each question you only see its number and type. Bet 0, 100, 200, 300 or 400, then the question appears and the tiles start moving.
  2. A correct answer adds the bet to your points. A wrong answer subtracts it. If your points drop below zero, the game is over.
  3. Answer fast. Every 5 seconds you wait, the reward for a correct answer loses 5% (down to 70% of the bet after 25 seconds). Wrong answers always cost the full bet.
  4. Streak bonus: answer three in a row and your total points are multiplied by 1.5.
  5. Press ? during a question to reveal its hint. The first look costs 50 points.
  6. Steer with ← → or A / D, press 1–4 to pick a lane, or tap and drag on the runway. Space or “Jump now” lands early. Answers are shuffled every game.
